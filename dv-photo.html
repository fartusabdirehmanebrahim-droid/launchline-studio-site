    The official photo rules we check

    • SizeSquare, 600×600 pixels (max 1200×1200)
    • FileJPEG, 240 KB or smaller
    • Head sizeChin to top of hair is 50–69% of the photo height
    • Eye positionEyes 56–69% up from the bottom of the photo
    • BackgroundPlain white or off-white, no shadows or patterns
    • ColourFull colour, not black and white, no filters
    • RecentTaken within the last 6 months
    • FaceFacing the camera, neutral expression, eyes open, no glasses

    Source: U.S. Department of State photo requirements at travel.state.gov. Always read the official instructions before you submit.

    Tips for a photo that passes

    • Stand 50 cm from a white wallThis avoids shadows behind your head.
    • Face a windowSoft daylight from the front, not from above or behind.
    • Ask someone else to take itHold the phone at eye level, 1–1.5 m away. Selfies distort the face.
    • Use the back cameraIt is sharper than the front camera.
