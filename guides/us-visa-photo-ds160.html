US visa photo for the DS-160: make it at home

When you fill in the DS-160 online application for a US tourist, student or work visa, you must upload a digital photo. If it does not meet the rules, the form can reject it or your appointment can be delayed.

What the DS-160 photo needs

  • Square JPEG, at least 600×600 pixels and at most 1200×1200.
  • File size 240 KB or less.
  • Colour photo on a plain white or off-white background.
  • Head (chin to top of hair) 50–69% of the image height; eyes 56–69% up from the bottom.
  • Recent (last 6 months), facing the camera, neutral expression, no glasses.

Step by step

  1. Stand in front of a white wall in daylight. Do not stand right against the wall, to avoid shadows.
  2. Ask someone to take the photo at eye level from about 1–1.5 metres, using the back camera.
  3. Open our photo tool, choose US visa (DS-160) and upload the photo.
  4. The tool centres your face, makes the 600×600 crop and checks the photo. If something is wrong, it tells you what to fix.
  5. Check the preview, pay $2 with card, EVC, ZAAD, Sahal, eDahab or Premier Wallet, and download your file.
