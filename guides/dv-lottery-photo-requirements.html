DV Lottery photo requirements: the complete checklist

A wrong photo is one of the most common reasons a Diversity Visa (DV) Lottery entry is disqualified. The rules are strict but simple once you see them in one place.

Status: the U.S. Department of State has not yet announced dates for the next DV Lottery registration. Prepare your photo early, but remember it must be taken within 6 months of the day you submit. Entry is free and only at dvprogram.state.gov.

The technical rules

RuleRequirement
SizeSquare, 600×600 pixels (up to 1200×1200)
FileJPEG (.jpg), 240 KB or smaller
ColourFull colour (24-bit), not black and white
Head sizeChin to top of hair = 50–69% of the photo height
Eye heightEyes 56–69% up from the bottom of the photo
BackgroundPlain white or off-white, no shadows or patterns

The face rules

  • Taken within the last 6 months and looks like you today.
  • Face the camera directly, neutral expression, both eyes open.
  • No glasses. No hats or head coverings, except religious ones worn daily, with the full face visible.
  • Only you in the photo. No filters, no editing.

The easy way to get it right

Stand about 50 cm in front of a white wall, face a window for soft light, and ask someone to take the photo with the phone's back camera at eye level. Then upload it to our tool: it finds your face automatically, crops it to 600×600 with the head and eyes in the correct position, compresses it under 240 KB and checks the background, colour, brightness and sharpness. You see a preview before you pay.

No tool can guarantee acceptance, because only the Department of State decides. But checking every technical rule removes the most common mistakes.
