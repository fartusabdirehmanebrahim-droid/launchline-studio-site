7 reasons DV Lottery photos get rejected

Every year many DV Lottery entries are disqualified because of the photo, even when everything else is correct. Here are the mistakes we see most and how to avoid them.

1. Wrong size or shape

The photo must be square, 600×600 pixels (up to 1200×1200). Phone photos are tall rectangles, so they must be cropped correctly, not stretched.

2. File too large

The JPEG must be 240 KB or smaller. Phone photos are often 2–5 MB, so they must be compressed without becoming blurry.

3. Head too small or too big

Your head (chin to top of hair) must fill 50–69% of the photo height, with your eyes 56–69% up from the bottom. Selfies taken too far away usually fail this.

4. Shadows or a busy background

The background must be plain white or off-white. Stand away from the wall and face a window so shadows don't fall behind you.

5. Glasses

Glasses are not allowed in the photo, even for people who wear them every day.

6. An old photo

The photo must be taken within 6 months of your entry. Do not reuse last year's DV photo.

7. Edited or filtered photos

Beauty filters, background removal apps and retouching can make the photo invalid. Use a natural, unedited photo.
